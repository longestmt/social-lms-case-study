Decision 01

Admin Portal

05 / 09

Shared administration addressed sales needs and specialist-dependent setup.

My contribution: Built the investment case with sales and engineering input.

1 · Problem

Setup depended on specialists

Fragile tools required product and engineering.

2 · Decision

Shared administration tools

Managed setup and customer self-service.

3 · Tradeoff

Delayed Tomcast features.

Tomcast already paid us to manage its instance.

4 · Observed result

Almost all managed implementation moved to the customer rep.

Stronger buyer response and customer self-service.

Decision 02

Learning Paths

06 / 09

An ordered sequence met the common prerequisite need.

My contribution: Customer conversations identified the need for fundamentals first.

1 · Request

Conditional branching

Complex engineering and admin UI.

2 · Decision

A prerequisite sequence

Complete one step to unlock the next.

3 · Tradeoff

Advanced conditions left out.

Implementation: a few days after discovery and scoping.

4 · Observed result

Positive demos, fewer sales escalations,
and new content discussions.

Qualitative signals. Conditional requests remained.

Decision 03

Rewards

07 / 09

Customer funding justified Rewards while core LMS work waited.

My contribution: Developed the concept with a designer and presented with the CEO and Account Executive.

1 · Problem

Customer funding for gamification

Core LMS needs competed for capacity.

2 · Decision

Redeemable social recognition

Peers gave coins for helpful forum posts.

3 · Tradeoff

Deferred course-standard updates (SCORM), infrastructure, and admin work.

4 · Observed result

Funding secured.

Uneven appeal. Stronger fit with sales-oriented teams.

CEO and Account Executive negotiated funding. Sales and marketing led targeting; I supported messaging and discovery.
