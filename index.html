Decision 01

Admin Portal

05 / 10

Shared administration moved routine implementation to a customer rep.

My contribution: Combined sales needs and engineering concerns into the investment case.

1 · Problem

Setup depended on specialists

Fragile tools required product and engineering.

2 · Decision

Build shared administration tools

Support managed setup and customer self-service.

3 · Tradeoff

Admin Portal sooner.
Tomcast features later.

Tomcast already paid us to manage its instance.

4 · Observed result

Almost all white-glove implementation moved to the customer rep.

Stronger buyer response and customer self-service.

Decision 02

Learning Paths

06 / 10

An ordered sequence met the common prerequisite need.

My contribution: Customer conversations revealed that learners needed fundamentals before dependent topics.

1 · Problem

The request: conditional branching

Complex engineering and administration UI.

2 · Decision

Build a prerequisite sequence

Complete one step to unlock the next.

3 · Tradeoff

Covered common prerequisites.
Left advanced conditions out.

Implementation took a few days, after discovery and scoping.

4 · Observed result

Positive demos, fewer sales escalations,
and new content discussions.

Qualitative signals. Some conditional requests remained.

Decision 03

Rewards

07 / 10

Customer funding justified Rewards while core LMS work waited.

My contribution: Developed the concept with a designer and presented with the CEO and Account Executive.

1 · Problem

Funding competed with core needs

Tomcast wanted to fund gamification.

2 · Decision

Make social recognition redeemable

Peers gave coins for helpful forum posts.

3 · Tradeoff

Deferred course-standard updates (SCORM), infrastructure, and admin work.

CEO and Account Executive negotiated the development contract.

4 · Observed result

Funding secured. Appeal was uneven, with stronger fit for sales-oriented teams.

Sales and marketing led targeting. I supported messaging and discovery.
